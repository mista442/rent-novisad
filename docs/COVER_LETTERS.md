# Сопроводительные сообщения

Отправляется **sr** или **en**; **ru** — перевод для вас. Перегенерация: `python -m rent_novisad docs`.


## Официальный (`formal`)

### Srpski (латиница)

```
Poštovani,
Zovem se ВАШЕ ИМЯ i zanima me Vaš oglas za izdavanje stana (Grbavica). Tražim stan za dugoročan najam u Novom Sadu.

Bio bih Vam zahvalan na odgovorima na sledeća pitanja:
1. Da li je stan još uvek slobodan i od kog datuma bi mogao da se useli (2026-10-06)?
2. Da li se izdaje na duži rok (najmanje 12 meseci) i kolika je mesečna kirija?
3. Da li su komunalni troškovi (struja, grejanje, voda, infostan) uključeni u cenu ili se plaćaju posebno? Koliko to otprilike iznosi zimi?
4. Da li se traži depozit i u kom iznosu? Da li ugovor ide preko overe (potpisi kod javnog beležnika)?
5. Da li je stan opremljen (nameštaj, šporet, frižider, veš mašina)? Da li sve radi ispravno?
6. Da li postoji internet (optika/kabl) i klima uređaj?
7. Da li je moguća prijava boravka na toj adresi? (potrebno mi je za boravišnu dozvolu)
8. Da li izdajete penzionerima? Ja sam penzioner, miran stanar.
9. Kada bih mogao da razgledam stan?

Unapred hvala na odgovoru.
Srdačan pozdrav,
ВАШЕ ИМЯ
```

### English

```
Dear Sir or Madam,
My name is ВАШЕ ИМЯ and I am interested in your advertisement for the apartment (Grbavica). I am looking for a long-term rental in Novi Sad.

I would be grateful if you could answer the following questions:
1. Is the apartment still available, and from what date could I move in (2026-10-06)?
2. Is it available for a long-term lease (at least 12 months), and what is the monthly rent?
3. Are utilities (electricity, heating, water, building fees) included in the rent or paid separately? Roughly how much are they in winter?
4. Is a deposit required, and how much? Will the lease be a formal written contract?
5. Is the apartment furnished (furniture, stove, fridge, washing machine)? Is everything in working order?
6. Is there internet (fibre/cable) and air conditioning?
7. Is it possible to register my residence at this address? (I need it for my residence permit)
8. Do you rent to retirees? I am a retiree and a quiet tenant.
9. When could I view the apartment?

Thank you in advance for your reply.
Kind regards,
ВАШЕ ИМЯ
```
